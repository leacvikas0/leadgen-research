# Agent 3 brief: WHAT SELLING ACTUALLY WORKS

## Your job
Research **what actually converts** when selling websites / web design / small digital presence to local businesses (primary: **India**), including cold and warm approaches.

Not theory decks. Lived evidence: agency posts, case studies with real constraints, cold email/WhatsApp/call patterns that work or fail, pricing psychology, offer structure.

## Questions
1. What **offers** convert? (fixed price site, "₹X in Y days", redesign, Google Business + site, booking site, ecom, WhatsApp catalog upgrade, landing page for ads)
2. What **angles** work? (OTA commission save, stop losing DMs, exam season leads, "competitors already have", mobile speed, one-page brochure, UPI/payment on site, multi-language)
3. What **channels** for first touch? (WhatsApp, call, IG DM, LinkedIn, email, walk-in, referral) — evidence of relative effectiveness for India local SMB
4. Message patterns that get replies vs get ignored/blocked
5. Proof that sells: before/after, local case study, video walkthrough, free audit, competitor screenshot
6. Pricing bands that close for small India sites (if data exists) — label uncertain
7. Objections: "my nephew does it", "Justdial enough", "Instagram enough", "too expensive", "I'll think" — rebuttals that work
8. What fails: long decks, English corporate tone, feature lists, "AI website in 5 min", spam volume
9. Relationship of **lead quality** to close rate (do better leads change the pitch?)
10. Ethical/compliance notes that affect conversion (trust, not being spammy) — brief
