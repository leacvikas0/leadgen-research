# Demand & Deep Benefits: Where India SMBs Actually Need Websites

**Agent 1 research · 2026-07-23**  
**Scope:** India-first local SMB / IG-first / hospitality / education / clinics / trades  
**Methods:** Web search + primary page reads; secondary global patterns labeled  
**Lens:** (A) Active hunting vs (B) Deep economic benefit — not “credibility” fluff

---

## 1. Executive take

**Highest-ranked demand for websites is where a site is a *margin machine* or a *conversion asset*, not a brochure.**

| Rank | Vertical | Why it wins |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **1** | **Resorts / homestays / boutique stays** | OTA commissions of ~12–20%+ of booking value are a pure margin leak. Direct booking site + WhatsApp/IG funnel has the cleanest ₹ math of any vertical. |
| **2** | **Wedding vendors (photo, MUA, decor, planners)** | Marketplace listings cost ₹50k–₹85k/year (vendor-reported) with shared, budget-mismatched leads. Site + IG portfolio is the owned pipeline vendors already say they need after burning listing money. |
| **3** | **IG/WhatsApp-first D2C / catalog sellers past ~₹5–10L+ monthly** | Pure WhatsApp works at small scale (even ₹30 Cr ARR anecdotes exist). At growth, abandoned DMs, no COD trust page, no SEO, ad-landing chaos, and ops drag create a real conversion + ops problem — site as commerce backend, not “presence.” |
| **4** | **Tuition / coaching / test prep (local + competitive)** | Parents search “coaching near me” → Maps pack; ads without landing pages burn money; batch admission forms and trust (results, fees, faculty) need a durable URL. |
| **5** | **Clinics / dentists / diagnostic labs (multi-doctor or specialty)** | GBP/Maps often beats a site for “near me,” but site is the booking landing + procedure trust page + Practo-escape path. Stronger when ticket size / trust stakes rise (dental implants, IVF, diagnostics). |
| **6** | **Real estate agents / channel partners** | Portal subs ₹6k–₹1.2L/mo with *shared* leads; exclusive Meta/Google systems need landing pages. Site is part of lead system, not vanity. |
| **7** | **Restaurants / cafes / cloud kitchens** | Swiggy/Zomato ~18–28% base commission (effective often 30%+ with fees) — deep pain — but **own-site ordering rarely replaces platforms** for discovery. Site is secondary (menu, events, direct for regulars) unless dine-in / brand / cloud-kitchen brand play. |
| **8** | **Local services (salon, gym, AC, interior)** | High volume of “no site” on Maps; weak ability-to-pay and WhatsApp-first booking. Deprioritize cold website sales unless premium positioning or multi-location. |

**Core thesis:** Sell websites where **(1) platform tax is measurable**, **(2) buyer journey already involves Google search**, or **(3) ads/listings need an owned landing page**. Kill pure “you need a website for credibility” pitches to hyperlocal trades who live on WhatsApp + Maps call button.

## 3. Active-hunting places (where people post “need website”)

Evidence of *active* “looking for designer” demand is thinner and noisier than deep-benefit math. Best hunting surfaces:

| Channel | What appears | Quality notes |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **r/FreelanceIndia, r/developersIndia, India freelance Discords** | Occasional “need website urgently” / founder posts | Mixed: startups, personal brands, one-off SMBs. Low volume vs global freelancing subs. |
| **Facebook groups** (city business, wedding vendors, restaurant owners, coaching owners) | “Anyone know web designer?”, redesign asks | High noise; good for wedding + coaching cities. |
| **IndiaMART / Justdial / Sulekha** | Buyers of “website designing services” categories | Intent mixed with tire-kickers; price-sensitive. |
| **Urban Company-style / local service demand N/A** | Not primary for websites | — |
| **Agency inbound keywords** | “website design [city]”, “Shopify expert India” | Demand exists commercially (many agencies compete) — **not proof of unmet SMB need**, but proves paid search competition. |
| **LinkedIn** | Founders hiring “need landing page for ads” | Higher quality for D2C + real estate lead systems. |
| **WedMeGood / vendor communities** | Complaints about listings → “building own pipeline” | Active pain, not always active website RFPs. |
| **Homestay host groups / Airbnb host India communities** | Direct booking discussions | Sell booking engine + site, not logo redesign. |
| **College / coaching Facebook pages comments** | Rare “how do I get more admissions” | Soft demand → education SEO package. |

**Practical implication:** For India local SMB web design, **outbound on deep-benefit verticals beats waiting for public RFPs.** Active hunting volume is insufficient alone to build a pipeline.

## 4. Deep-benefit math (with sources)

### 4.1 Hospitality OTA escape (highest clarity)

**Commission ranges (India-focused host/industry content, 2025–2026):**

| Platform | Typical host-side cut | Source type |
|----------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Booking.com** | ~15% standard; **up to ~18% in India** / higher with Preferred/Genius programs (~17–20%) | Homestay host guides (Homeyhuts Jul 2025; Fursat Jun 2026) |
| **MakeMyTrip / Goibibo** | **~12–20%** depending on property type / negotiation | Same |
| **Airbnb** | Split model (~3% host + guest fee) **or** host-only ~**14–16%** | Same |
| **Niche India platforms** | e.g. Homeyhuts claims ~10–12% (competitor; treat carefully) | Homeyhuts own content |

**Illustrative math (not a guarantee — use as pitch model):**

- Homestay: ₹8,000/night × 120 OTA nights/year = ₹9,60,000 GBV  
- At **15% OTA** = **₹1,44,000/year** commission  
- If **25% of nights shift direct** (30 nights) via site + past-guest WhatsApp + Google: save **~₹36,000/year** at 15%  
- If **50% of nights direct** over 2 years of brand building: save **~₹72,000+/year**  
- Site + basic booking tool cost (India SMB ballpark) often **₹25k–₹1.5L** one-time + small annual — **payback plausible inside one peak season** for multi-room properties. [uncertain exact payback without property data]

**Guest-side proof of direct discounting:** Travelers report hotels quoting **~15–20% lower** off OTA when called direct (LinkedIn/travel anecdotes) — hotels share part of saved commission.

**Mechanism for site:**  
`Google/IG/past guest → property site with live rates → pay online or WhatsApp confirm → optional channel manager sync`  
Not: “pretty photos only.”

### 4.2 Food delivery commission (pain high; website ROI ambiguous)

From 2025 industry write-ups (e.g. Spice Advisors May 2025):

- Base commission **Swiggy & Zomato: ~18–28%**  
- Plus delivery platform cuts, **18% GST on commission**, ads, early settlement fees  
- Example: ₹300 dish → restaurant may net **~₹190–195** before food cost (~**35%+** platform stack)

**Nikhil Kamath clip / F&B commentary:** delivery commissions discussed in **~24–28%** “norm” range (secondary media).

**Website implication:** Direct ordering (website/QR/WhatsApp) recovers margin **only for customers you already own**. Discovery still platforms. Best pitch: **repeat-guest + office park + catering**, not “replace Zomato.”

### 4.3 Wedding marketplace tax

- WedMeGood: **80,000+** active vendors (company About)  
- Premium listings **vendor-reported ₹50,000–₹85,000** for 6–12 months, often EMI, **non-refundable** (Weddingkart synthesis Jun 2026; vendor terms cited)  
- Public failure cases: photographer **₹50k, zero conversions**; MUA **~₹11.8k wasted, &lt;1 lead in 9 months** (navdeepsoni.com; Quora cited by Weddingkart)  
- WeddingPro 2025 survey (global n=531): most pros convert **21–60% of real inquiries**; **budget mismatch** top kill reason  

**Website math:**  
One mid wedding photo booking (e.g. ₹40–80k package) can **pay for a strong portfolio site**. Listing is rented demand; site is SEO + IG pin + PDF replacement + package filter before WhatsApp.

### 4.4 Real estate portal + landing pages

Optimized Growth (Apr 2026, industry blog — **not audited financials**):

| Portal plan (approx) | Monthly cost | Leads | CPL | Brokers sharing lead |
|----------------------|--------------|-------|-----|----------------------|
| MagicBricks Basic tier-2 | ₹8–15k | 25–45 | ₹280–600 | 3–7 |
| MagicBricks Premium metro | ₹20–40k | 45–90 | ₹400–900 | 6–12 |
| 99acres Premium metro | ₹18–35k | 40–80 | ₹350–900 | 5–12 |
| Multi-portal common | ₹35–80k | 100–200 | ₹350–800 | 4–12 |

Exclusive Meta leads: **₹800–3,000** CPL by city; conversion to deal often much higher than shared portal leads **if** landing page + speed-to-WhatsApp exist.

**Website role:** Project microsite / broker brand site as **ad landing + inventory + trust**, not brochure for “find me on Google” alone.

### 4.5 D2C conversion economics

BYB Traction / India Shopify CRO (Apr 2026, agency benchmarks — directional):

- Good India CR: **1.5–2.5%**; underperformers **0.3–0.8%**  
- COD can be **20–50%** of orders; hiding COD kills conversions  
- WhatsApp cart recovery open rates claimed **70–90%** vs email **15–25%**  

**Pure WhatsApp counter-case:** LinkedIn anecdote (Arjun Vaidya) of **₹30 Cr ARR WhatsApp-only** D2C — proves site is not mandatory at all scales. Site demand spikes when:

1. Meta ads need scalable landing  
2. Catalog &gt; DM capacity  
3. Multi-SKU + COD logistics need structured checkout  
4. Brand wants SEO / Amazon-independent discovery  

### 4.6 Coaching admissions

Classpro / local SEO agency content (2025–2026): parents discover via **Google + Maps + referrals**; weak GMB = silent loss. Google Ads without landing page = wasted spend (agency case narrative: “no website or landing page” as gap).

**Seasonality:** board results, NEET/JEE cycles, new academic year — inquiry spikes favor forms + result pages + fee FAQs.

### 4.7 Healthcare / Practo

- India clinic content: **GBP often drives more patients than website/social** (Deep Aarogya, GMBPro India guides 2026)  
- Practo: official help text describes **technology fees to clinics**, not simple public commission rate card; Reddit/competitors claim **₹200–250 per consult** or **25–30%** — **conflicting; mark uncertain**  
- Site value strongest as: **GBP appointment URL target**, treatment education pages, multi-location brand

## 5. When they do NOT need a website

| Situation | Why site is low priority |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| **Hyperlocal service with phone-first demand** (AC repair, plumber, tiffin) | Customer journey = Maps call / WhatsApp. GMB + reviews win. |
| **Micro IG seller** (&lt;~50–100 orders/mo, one person ops) | WA catalog + UPI sufficient; site is ops overhead. |
| **Restaurant 90%+ delivery platform dependent** with no brand / no dine-in | Own site won’t fill discovery hole. |
| **Solo tutor / home tuition** | GMB + WhatsApp enough unless running multi-batch institute. |
| **Broker who only gets builder walk-ins / CP inventory** | Portals optional; website rarely first lever. |
| **Business already has excellent GBP + Practo + full appointment software** | Incremental site ROI thin until multi-service SEO. |
| **Owner won’t update content or answer form leads** | Dead site hurts more than no site. |
| **Price floor shoppers wanting ₹3–5k “complete website”** | Not quality ICP; churn + scope hell. |

**Counter-signal to Maps scrapers:** “No websiteUri” is a **weak quality signal**. Ability to pay + platform tax + search intent + seasonality are stronger.

## 8. Sales implications (demand-side only)

1. **Lead with ₹ math**, not “professional image”: OTA %, listing spend, portal CPL, ad landings.  
2. **Vertical pitch order:** Homestays/hotels → wedding vendors → scaling D2C → coaching institutes → RE channel partners with ad spend → specialty clinics.  
3. **Bundle:** Site + WhatsApp CTAs + Google Business appointment/link + basic analytics. Pure brochure = low close.  
4. **Qualify ability:** Already paying OTA/listing/portal/ads = can pay for site. Maps-only AC guy = usually cannot/will not.  
5. **Seasonal outbound:** Homestays pre-peak; wedding vendors pre-Nov; coaching pre-session; D2C pre-festive.
