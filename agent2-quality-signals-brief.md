# Agent 2 brief: HIGH-QUALITY LEAD SIGNALS (beyond blind Maps)

## Your job
Research **how to find SUPER high-quality leads** for selling websites — methods and **composite signals** better than "Google Maps listing with empty website field."

Blind Maps hunting is assumed weak. Focus on **quality filters** and **intent/timing** signals.

## Quality definition (use this)
A quality lead has:
1. **NEED** — real gap (no site, broken site, IG-only, OTA-only, bad mobile site)
2. **ABILITY** — can pay (revenue proxies: busy IG, many reviews, ads spend, staff, multi-location, premium positioning)
3. **TIMING** — reason to act now (season, complaints, expansion, bad reviews about discovery, lost ranking, new competitor, hiring, festival, property launch)
4. **REACH** — WhatsApp/phone/IG DM possible without illegal spam architecture lectures

Score mindset: NEED × ABILITY × TIMING, not "has phone number."

## Research questions
1. What signals do good agencies / GTM operators use for local/web-design leads?
2. What public digital footprints indicate ability to pay in India SMBs?
3. Intent signals: where do "I need a website" / redesign / "someone make me a site" appear?
4. Composite formulas used in practice (even if informal) — e.g. "IG shop + product catalog + no .in domain + >5k followers"
5. Negative signals (tire-kickers, just started, no reviews, price shoppers only)
6. Non-Maps sources: Reddit, IG hashtags, Justdial/Sulekha, Google reviews text, LinkedIn, job posts ("need website" for their business vs hiring developer), directories, WhatsApp Business catalogs, YouTube channels without site, App store listings, etc.
7. How "no website" is validated better than Maps empty field (parked domain, Instagram linktree-only, broken SSL, last updated 2019, etc.)
